Method

Each criterion was standardized to a landslide susceptibility score and combined using a weighted overlay. The final Landslide Potential Index was grouped into five percentile-based classes.

Baseline

LULC reference: 2021. Rainfall intensity: mean annual maximum 1-day rainfall for 2015–2024. Final analysis grid: 250 m.
