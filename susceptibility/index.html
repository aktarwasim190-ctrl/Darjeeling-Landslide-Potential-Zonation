Assignment 8 — Method & Validation
Weighted Overlay

Fifteen susceptibility criteria were standardized to 1–5 scores and combined using weights totaling 100%.

Existing Landslides

54 inventory points were split into 36 model points and 18 independent validation points.

Validation Result

10 of 18 validation landslides fall in High + Very High zones; 15 of 18 fall in Moderate or higher zones.

Final Output

The 250 m Landslide Susceptibility Index is classified as Very Low, Low, Moderate, High and Very High.
